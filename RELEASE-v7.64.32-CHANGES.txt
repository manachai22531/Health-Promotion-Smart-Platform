HealthCheck v7.64.32

  initial password and must change it before obtaining a normal staff session.
- Individual reset restores the configured initial credential and revokes sessions
  and pending change tokens. Disabled accounts remain disabled.

  focus stays inside the dialog.

  as the patient save. Existing/historical confirmations receive no retroactive EXP.
- Levels 1–100; next-level cost is 100 + 50*(current level-1) EXP. Ten shield ranks
  change every ten levels; level 100 retains lifetime EXP and case totals.
- Previous v7.64.31 cursor small/medium/large fixes retained.
